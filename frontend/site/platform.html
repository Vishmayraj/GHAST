From anomaly to incident

What happens after the flag.

A flagged anomaly doesn't reach an analyst as a bare number. A bounded, tool-using agent gathers evidence, forms a hypothesis, and either drafts a structured report or escalates — and every step it takes is logged.

The investigation agent

Bounded, evidence-gathering, auditable

The agent never declares "this is spoofing." It pulls trajectory history, checks known jamming zones, checks incident history, and scores its own confidence in a hypothesis — jamming, targeted spoof, equipment fault, or benign. Escalation is driven by that confidence, not by raw anomaly magnitude, which is the specific answer to the false-positive risk an insurer buyer will actually judge this on.
