GHAST

GNSS and AIS Hazardous Spoofing Tracker

Watches vessel position reports, flags the ones that do not add up, and shows an analyst why.

A ship reports where it is. GHAST checks whether that is possible.

Every vessel broadcasts its position, speed and course. When that signal is jammed or faked, the numbers stop fitting together: a turn too sharp for the hull, a jump with no time to make it, a position frozen while the speed says the ship is moving.

GHAST learns how each vessel normally moves and flags the reports that break from it. Then it gathers the evidence, says what it thinks happened and how sure it is, and leaves the decision to a person.
