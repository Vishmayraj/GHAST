What just happened

A trajectory, not a signature.

GHAST doesn't watch for a known jamming signature or a blocklist of bad actors. It watches how a vessel moves — speed, course, heading, rate of turn — and asks whether the next reported position is physically plausible for that vessel. When the answer is no by a wide enough margin, that's the signal.
